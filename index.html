Discreet use

Turn on Discreet in a session and the screen goes near-blank — a faint grey ring, no words, no colour. The cue is what you feel, not what you see: one buzz to squeeze, two short buzzes to release. You can put the phone face-down in your lap and just follow the buzzes.
iPhone note: Safari doesn't allow web vibration. The app falls back to a soft audio blip — put in one earbud and nobody hears a thing. Set your cue style in Settings.
