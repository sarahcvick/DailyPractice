Discreet use

Turn on Discreet in a session and the screen goes near-blank — two greys, no words, no colour. Reading it at a glance:
Hold — the darker, thicker ring winding clockwise, filling up.
Rest — a pale, thin ring unwinding back to nothing.
Direction alone tells you the phase, so you don't have to focus on it.
iPhone note: no browser on iOS can vibrate — Apple doesn't expose it to web apps. Use the soft blip with one earbud in, or go by the ring alone. Set your cue in Settings.
